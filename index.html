Submission Type CHOOSE ONE

🛡
Warranty
Repair completed within the brand warranty period.
📝
Pre-Approval
Repair NOT finalized yet. Recommended for high part values or doubts on acceptance of specific jobs.
🤝
Goodwill / Commercial Support
Vehicle out of warranty, but with grounds for the brand to support the repair.
💡 Advice: open a Technical Support Ticket with the brand before requesting pre-approval — it strengthens the technical grounds of the request. Fill the ticket number in step 03 (Brand Codes & References). Do not finalize the repair before approval. RO conclusion, parts invoice and new/installed part photos are not requested at this stage.
💡 Goodwill requires: sale date + current km (to quantify how far out of warranty), failure date, customer name, and Support Docs (service history / evidence backing the request). The stronger the justification, the higher the chance the brand supports it.
